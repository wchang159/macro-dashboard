📜 Historical Market Crashes & Recessions Reference

Understanding how indicators behaved during past crises helps contextualize current readings. Below are key indicator levels during major market events.

💥 2008 Financial Crisis (Sep 2008 - Mar 2009)
Event: Lehman Brothers collapse triggered global financial system seizure. S&P 500 fell 57% peak-to-trough.
Indicator Peak Crisis Level Normal Range Signal
VIX 89.5 (Oct 2008) 15-25 🔴 Extreme panic
HY Spreads ~2,000 bps (Dec 2008) 300-500 bps 🔴 Credit market frozen
IG Spreads ~650 bps 100-200 bps 🔴 Investment grade stress
Unemployment 10.0% (Oct 2009) 4-5% 🔴 Deep recession
2s10s Yield Curve Inverted -0.5% (2007) +50 to +150 bps 🔴 Predicted recession 12M prior
3M10Y Yield Curve Inverted -1.5% (2007) +100 to +200 bps 🔴 Strong recession signal
Fed Balance Sheet $900B → $2.2T expansion ~$800B pre-crisis 🟢 Emergency QE response
Oil (WTI) $147 → $35 $70-85 🔴 Demand collapse
Copper $4.00 → $1.30 $3.50-4.50 🔴 Global recession signal
Key Lesson: Yield curve inverted 12-18 months before recession. Credit spreads exploded first (early warning). VIX above 40 marked capitulation/buying opportunities. Fed balance sheet expansion signaled intervention floor.
🔴 2000-2002 Dot-Com Crash
Event: Tech bubble burst. NASDAQ fell 78% from peak. S&P 500 down 49%.
Indicator Crisis Level Normal Range Signal
VIX Peak ~45 (Sep 2001) 15-25 🔴 Post 9/11 spike
Unemployment 6.3% (Jun 2003) 4-5% 🟡 Mild recession
ISM Manufacturing 41.2 (Sep 2001) Above 50 🔴 Deep contraction
Yield Curve Inverted 2000 Positive 🔴 Recession predictor
HY Spreads ~1,000 bps 300-500 bps 🔴 Credit stress
Key Lesson: Asset-specific bubble (tech) without systemic credit crisis = shallower recession. Manufacturing PMI fell below 42 = severe downturn. Recovery took 2.5 years.
🦠 2020 COVID-19 Crash (Feb-Mar 2020)
Event: Pandemic-driven global lockdown. Fastest 30% decline in history (22 days). V-shaped recovery due to unprecedented stimulus.
Indicator Crisis Level Normal Range Signal
VIX 82.7 (Mar 16, 2020) 15-25 🔴 Second highest ever
HY Spreads ~1,100 bps 300-500 bps 🔴 Credit crunch
Unemployment 14.7% (Apr 2020) 4-5% 🔴 Highest since Great Depression
Oil (WTI) -$37 (negative!) $70-85 🔴 Demand destruction
ISM Manufacturing 41.5 (Apr 2020) Above 50 🔴 Shutdown impact
Fed Balance Sheet $4T → $7T+ (3 months) $4T pre-COVID 🟢 Unprecedented expansion
M2 Money Supply +25% YoY growth 5-7% growth 🟢 Massive stimulus
10Y Treasury 0.54% (Mar 2020) 2-3% 🔴 Flight to safety
Key Lesson: Exogenous shock with NO advance warning from typical indicators. Fed/fiscal response unprecedented. VIX spike marked exact bottom. Liquidity injection = V-shaped recovery. Not a typical business cycle recession.
⚫ 1987 Black Monday (Oct 19, 1987)
Event: Single-day 22.6% S&P 500 crash. Portfolio insurance + program trading cascade. No recession followed.
Indicator Crisis Level Signal
S&P 500 -22.6% in ONE day 🔴 Largest single-day drop ever
VIX (retroactive) Est. ~150+ 🔴 Off the charts
Economy Remained strong 🟢 Technical crash, not fundamental
Fed Response Immediate liquidity 🟢 "Fed put" established
Key Lesson: Market crashes can happen without recession if fundamentals intact. Program trading/technical factors can create crashes. Fed intervention critical. Demonstrated importance of circuit breakers (implemented after).
⚠️ 2022 Bear Market (Jan-Oct 2022)
Event: Fed tightening cycle. Inflation surge. S&P 500 down 25%, NASDAQ down 35%. No recession (yet).

🏚️ 2011 European Debt Crisis
Event: Greek sovereign debt crisis. Contagion fears. S&P 500 down 19% (near bear market).
Indicator Crisis Level Signal

📊 Pattern Recognition Across Crashes
Early Warning Signals (6-18 months ahead):
  • Yield curve inversion (2s10s or 3m10y) - preceded ALL recessions except 2020 (exogenous)
  • ISM Manufacturing below 48 for 3+ months
  • Credit spreads (HY) starting to widen from lows
  • Unemployment rising 0.5%+ from 12-month low (Sahm Rule)
Acute Crisis Signals (immediate):
  • VIX above 40 = panic/capitulation (often marks bottoms)
  • HY spreads above 1,000 bps = credit market dysfunction
  • Fed emergency meetings/interventions = systemic risk
  • Copper down 30%+ = global demand collapse
Recovery Signals:
  • Fed balance sheet expansion = liquidity floor
  • Credit spreads tightening from peaks
  • VIX declining from 40+ back to 20s
  • ISM Manufacturing back above 50
📊 Data Sources & Methodology
FRED API (via CORS proxy): Fed balance sheet, M2, RRP, TGA, unemployment, jobless claims, CPI, PPI, ISM data - loaded in parallel
Yahoo Finance (via CORS proxy): VIX, S&P 500, Treasury yields (2Y/10Y), equities, FX, commodities, HYG/LQD credit ETFs, 52-week drawdown calculation
Update Frequency: Daily for macro data (FRED, delayed 1 day), real-time for market data (Yahoo Finance, 15-min delay)
Signal Methodology: Historical percentile-based thresholds (10-year ranges). Red = top/bottom 20%, Yellow = 20-40%, Green = normal range
Drawdown Calculation: (Current Price - 52-Week High) / 52-Week High × 100. Updated real-time with market data.
Caching: All successfully loaded data cached locally for 24 hours. Opens instantly with cached data, then refreshes in background.
CORS Proxy: Uses api.allorigins.win to bypass browser restrictions. Both FRED and Yahoo data routed through proxy for reliability.
